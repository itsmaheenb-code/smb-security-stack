If your team is still sharing logins over Slack or a spreadsheet, a business password manager is one of the cheapest security upgrades available — most plans run a few dollars per seat per month. Here's how the three most commonly recommended options compare for a small team.

Tool Business price Best for
1Password Business ~$7.99/user/month (annual); Teams Starter Pack $19.95/mo flat for up to 10 users Best admin console and integrations; worth the premium for teams that use SSO/device management
NordPass Business ~$3.99/user/month (annual) Lowest cost per seat with solid core features
Bitwarden Teams / Enterprise $4/user/month (Teams); $6/user/month (Enterprise) Open-source, self-hostable option; strong choice for technical teams

1Password Business

1Password costs more per seat than the alternatives, but the gap tends to close once you factor in admin features: detailed usage reports, SSO integration (Okta, Azure AD, Google Workspace), and a Watchtower dashboard that flags weak or reused passwords across the whole team. For a team past about 15-20 people, or one already using SSO for other tools, the admin overhead saved usually justifies the price difference.

NordPass Business

NordPass is the cheapest of the three at roughly $3.99/user/month annually, and covers the fundamentals — shared vaults, activity logs, admin controls — without 1Password's deeper SSO/reporting layer. For a small team that mainly needs secure password sharing without heavy IT overhead, it's a reasonable default.

Check NordPass Business pricing

Bitwarden Teams / Enterprise

Bitwarden is open-source, which matters to some technical teams for auditability, and it can be self-hosted if you'd rather not store vault data with a third party at all. Pricing sits between the other two ($4/user for Teams, $6/user for Enterprise with SSO), and functionality is comparable to NordPass with a more developer-friendly ecosystem.

See Bitwarden's business pricing

Which one should you pick?

Team of 15+ already using SSO elsewhere, or want the strongest admin reporting: 1Password. Small team, cost-sensitive, want the basics done well: NordPass. Technical team that wants open-source or self-hosting as an option: Bitwarden. All three beat the "no password manager" status quo by a wide margin — the biggest jump in security here is adopting any of them, not which one you pick.
