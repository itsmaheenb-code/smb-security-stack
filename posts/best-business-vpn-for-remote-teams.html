A consumer VPN app isn't built for a team — it doesn't manage who has access to what, doesn't log activity, and doesn't integrate with your identity provider. Business VPN and zero-trust network access (ZTNA) tools solve a different problem: controlling exactly which internal resources each employee or contractor can reach, from anywhere.

FAQ

What's the difference between a VPN and zero-trust network access (ZTNA)?

A traditional VPN gives a connected device access to your whole internal network. ZTNA grants access resource-by-resource — an employee only reaches the specific apps or servers they're authorized for, which limits the damage if one device or account is compromised.

Do I need a business VPN if my team already uses a consumer VPN app?

A consumer VPN just masks your location/IP for browsing — it has no concept of your team, no access controls, and no admin visibility. If you need to control which employees or contractors can reach specific internal systems, that requires a business VPN or ZTNA tool, not a consumer app.

Is Tailscale actually free for a small team?

Tailscale offers a free tier for up to 3 users, which is enough to evaluate it or cover a very small team. Beyond that, it moves to per-user paid pricing — check their current pricing page for exact rates.

What happens to Perimeter 81 customers after the Check Point rebrand?

Perimeter 81 was acquired by Check Point and rebranded as Check Point Harmony SASE. Existing functionality continues under the new name as part of Check Point's broader SASE platform, now bundled with firewall-as-a-service and secure web gateway features.
