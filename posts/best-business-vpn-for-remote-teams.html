Best Business VPN for Remote Teams (2026)

A consumer VPN app isn't built for a team — it doesn't manage who has access to what, doesn't log activity, and doesn't integrate with your identity provider. Business VPN and zero-trust network access (ZTNA) tools solve a different problem: controlling exactly which internal resources each employee or contractor can reach, from anywhere.

Tool Pricing model Best for
NordLayer Per-user subscription, tiered plans Straightforward setup for a small-to-mid team, less enterprise complexity
Check Point Harmony SASE (formerly Perimeter 81) Per-user subscription, enterprise-leaning Teams that want a full SASE platform (ZTNA + firewall-as-a-service + secure web gateway) in one
Tailscale Free up to 3 users; paid tiers per-user beyond that Technical teams that want a fast, minimal-config mesh VPN (built on WireGuard)

NordLayer

NordLayer is built specifically for the small-to-mid-size business segment that finds full enterprise SASE platforms overkill — it covers the core zero-trust access controls, device posture checks, and network segmentation without a long enterprise sales process to get started.

Check NordLayer pricing

Check Point Harmony SASE (formerly Perimeter 81)

Following Check Point's acquisition of Perimeter 81, the product is now Check Point Harmony SASE — a fuller platform that bundles zero-trust network access with firewall-as-a-service and a secure web gateway. It's a bigger tool than most 10-person teams need on day one, but it's the option to consider if you expect to outgrow a basic VPN within the next year or two and don't want to re-platform.

See Check Point Harmony SASE

Tailscale

Tailscale takes a different approach: it's a mesh VPN built on WireGuard that connects your team's devices directly to each other and to internal servers, with minimal configuration. It's free for up to 3 users, which makes it an easy way to try zero-trust networking before committing to a paid platform, and it's popular with engineering-heavy teams for exactly that reason.

See Tailscale's pricing

Which one should you pick?

Want a clean, small-business-focused setup without enterprise sales calls: NordLayer. Expect to need a full SASE platform (firewall + web gateway + ZTNA together) as you grow: Check Point Harmony SASE. Technical team, want to start free and self-serve: Tailscale.
