Formatting is the last thing standing between a finished manuscript and a published book — and it's where a lot of first-time self-publishers either overpay a freelancer for something software can now do, or waste a weekend fighting Word's paragraph styles. Here's how the four tools most indie authors actually consider stack up.

Atticus

Atticus is a one-time $147 purchase that works on Windows, Mac, Linux, and Chromebook because it runs in the browser. Unlike Vellum, it doubles as a writing tool, not just a formatter, and it handles nonfiction (footnotes, endnotes, indexes) noticeably better than Vellum does. For most indie authors who aren't on a Mac, this is the default recommendation.

Vellum

Vellum produces some of the best-looking print interiors in the indie world, but it's Mac-only, and it's the most expensive option here — $199.99 for ebook formatting, $249.99 if you also want print. If you already own a Mac and print quality matters more to you than price, it's still the tool most professional formatters reach for themselves.

See Vellum's current pricing

Reedsy Studio

Formerly called the Reedsy Book Editor, Reedsy Studio is free, browser-based, and produces a genuinely professional EPUB and print-ready PDF with no design experience required. The tradeoff is fewer customization options than Atticus or Vellum — but if your budget is $0, this is the best free formatting tool available right now.

Try Reedsy Studio

Draft2Digital's free formatter

If you're already planning to distribute wide (Apple Books, Kobo, B&N, libraries) through Draft2Digital, their built-in formatting tool is free and integrated directly into your distribution workflow — you format and publish in the same place. It has fewer layout styles than Reedsy Studio but is the most convenient option if D2D is already your distributor.

Which one should you pick?

Zero budget and just need it done: Reedsy Studio. Windows/Linux and want more control plus a writing tool: Atticus. Mac owner who wants the best possible print interior and doesn't mind paying more: Vellum. Already distributing wide through D2D: use their built-in formatter and save a step.
