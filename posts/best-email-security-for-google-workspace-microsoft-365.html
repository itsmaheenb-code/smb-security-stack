FAQ

Isn't built-in Gmail/Outlook spam filtering enough?

Built-in filtering catches most bulk spam and known malware well. What it's weaker at is targeted phishing and business email compromise — a convincing fake invoice or a spoofed request from "your CEO" — which is exactly what dedicated email security add-ons focus on catching.

Does it matter if I'm on Google Workspace vs. Microsoft 365?

All three tools compared here support both platforms, so your choice isn't platform-locked. Confirm current supported integration methods (API-based vs. mail-gateway) for your specific platform before buying, since deployment approach affects setup time and what the tool can see.

What's the difference between API-based and gateway-based email security?

Gateway-based tools sit in front of your mail server and inspect messages before they're delivered, which can add a small delay but catches threats pre-delivery. API-based tools connect directly to your mailbox (Gmail/Outlook) and scan after delivery, which is faster to deploy and can also catch internal and post-delivery threats, but by definition acts slightly after the fact.

Is this worth it for a 5-10 person company?

Business email compromise scams (fake wire transfer or vendor payment requests) don't require a large target — a small company's finance or ops person is just as reachable as an enterprise one. If your team handles any payments or sensitive client data over email, the per-user cost is low relative to the cost of one successful scam.
