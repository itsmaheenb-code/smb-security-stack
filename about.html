About

The SMB Security Stack compares the security and backup software small businesses and remote teams actually use — password managers, business VPNs, cloud backup, and related tools. Most coverage of this space is either aimed at consumers or aimed at large enterprise IT — the small business owner or five-person remote team is usually an afterthought. That's the gap this site tries to fill.

Recommendations here are based on public information — pricing, admin features, platform support, and what each tool is actually built to handle at small-team scale — compared side by side for a specific use case. Where we haven't personally used a tool, we say so rather than implying hands-on testing that didn't happen.
