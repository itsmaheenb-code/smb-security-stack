About

The Indie Author Stack compares the software and services independent authors use to write, edit, format, publish, and market their books. Self-publishing has a lot of tool options and not much honest, specific comparison between them — that's the gap this site tries to fill.

Recommendations here are based on public information — pricing, features, platform support, and what each tool is actually built to do — compared side by side for a specific use case. Where we haven't personally used a tool, we say so rather than implying hands-on testing that didn't happen.
